Frequently asked questions

What happens if I miss an installment?

You can pay a missed installment along with the next one. Speak to your branch advisor if you need to pause or restructure your plan.

Can I redeem at any Regal Jewellers branch?

Yes — all schemes are redeemable at any of our branches across Kerala on presentation of your enrollment reference and ID proof.

Is my gold rate locked in advance?

Only Swarnaraksha locks a rate in advance. Other schemes redeem at the gold rate applicable on the day of purchase, plus any scheme bonus.

Can I switch schemes after enrolling?

Yes, subject to branch approval and any applicable adjustment — contact your enrollment branch to discuss switching.
