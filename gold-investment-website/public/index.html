Frequently asked questions

What exactly am I buying?

24K 999-purity digital gold. Every rupee you invest is converted to grams at the day's published rate and credited to your account balance.

What documents do I need to enroll?

KYC is mandatory: your Aadhaar number and PAN with a copy of each document (JPG, PNG or PDF), plus your email address and mobile number. Our team verifies your KYC within 24 hours of registration.

Are there any taxes or charges?

Yes — 3% GST applies on the purchase value of every gold purchase. At scheme closure, if you redeem as gold coins, a 1% making charge on the coins applies.

Is there a minimum investment?

Yes — ₹100 per purchase (plus 3% GST). There is no upper limit and no fixed schedule; top up whenever you like.

When and how can I redeem?

This is an 11-month scheme. After 11 months from your first purchase, you can close the scheme and redeem your accumulated gold as gold coins (1% making charge applies). If you don't wish to take coins, you can withdraw the equivalent value in money instead.

How do I track my holdings?

Log in to your account dashboard to see every purchase — the amount invested, the rate at which you bought, grams credited — and your gold's current value at today's rate.
